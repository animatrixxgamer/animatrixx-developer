About

A one-person studio that works like a small agency.

Animatrixx Developer is me — a student developer in Ondo State. I work solo, which means you talk to the person writing the code: written scope and a fixed price before any work starts, a preview you can click before anything goes live, and a straight answer when something isn't ready yet.

I started with Python automation and messaging bots, then moved into full web applications. That path shaped how I work: I care what happens when a form fails, when a payment arrives twice, or when a page opens on a slow phone in Ondo — not only how the homepage looks on a big screen.

The school platform below isn't a portfolio exercise. It handles admissions, fees and report cards, so a payment must never be recorded twice and a pupil taken off the roll must lose access immediately. That kind of detail is most of the work.

How I work: written scope before payment, a deposit on larger jobs, and no feature promised until I've run it myself. If something doesn't work yet, I say so.

What I don't take on yet

Honestly, not everything should be built by a one-person studio learning as it goes. I turn down banking or payment-only systems, authentication-heavy platforms for strangers' money, and 24/7 server support — those need security review and a support team behind them.

How a job runs

You tell me the scope, I put it in writing with a price and a date. A deposit starts the work, you see a preview before launch, and the balance is paid on delivery. Changes outside the agreed scope are quoted separately.
